# Research Ethics Statement

## What this dataset is

A collection of publicly posted content and public profile identifiers, gathered in connection with research into manufactured engagement. Public origin does not remove ethical obligations. The identifiers resolve to real, mostly private individuals, and the dataset's framing (deceptive practice, FTC rule violation) attaches a serious implication to inclusion.

## Principles this project follows

**1. Observations are published; verdicts are not.** The dataset and every analysis script in this repository output measurable facts and statistical flags. No component publishes a per-person determination that an individual broke a rule or a law. Whether specific conduct violates 16 CFR Part 465 is a legal question for regulators and courts, not a field in a JSON file.

**2. Aggregate first.** Published findings describe distributions, clusters, and patterns. Where an example is needed, prefer paraphrase and aggregate description over reproducing an identifiable record. Do not publish ranked "worst offender" lists of named individuals derived from this data.

**3. The inclusion claim keeps its tier.** Every person in this dataset is here because of a STATED collection criterion that cannot be independently verified from the file. Presenting inclusion as confirmed misconduct would overstate the evidence and could harm people wrongly captured by the collection process. See [Limitations](limitations.md), items 1 and 10.

**4. No CLEAN verdicts either.** The obligation runs both directions. This project never certifies an account as free of manipulation; absence of detected anomaly is reported as INCONCLUSIVE. A certification would be an evidence claim the data cannot support, and it would convert the project into a reputational rating service, which it is not.

**5. Minimal republication.** This repository does not contain the dataset. The raw file, with its personal identifiers and full post text, lives on Harvard Dataverse under that record's terms of use, where access, licensing, and takedown are governed by an institution built for research data stewardship. Analysis outputs committed here are aggregates.

**6. A person in the data can challenge it.** The [correction process](corrections.md) gives anyone represented in the dataset a documented path to dispute factual errors, request review of their inclusion, or flag records that misattribute content. Corrections are logged in version history.

**7. Copyright restraint.** Post text belongs to its authors. Research use here is analysis of patterns across a corpus. Downstream users should not republish substantial post text and should quote at most minimally for scholarly critique.

## Regulatory context

The FTC's Trade Regulation Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (16 CFR Part 465, effective October 2024) prohibits, among other things, the purchase or sale of fake indicators of social media influence. That rule is the reason this phenomenon matters commercially and legally (CORROBORATED: published in the Federal Register). It is not evidence about any particular account in this dataset.
